The people behind the work.

Principal investigator

Fernanda G. De Felice

Dr. De Felice's research explores the interface between Alzheimer's disease and metabolism, with pioneering work on hormone signaling, insulin resistance, and inflammation in the brain. Her studies, including recent findings on the exercise hormone irisin, have shaped the understanding of how lifestyle interventions, particularly physical exercise, can help preserve brain health and delay Alzheimer's disease.
