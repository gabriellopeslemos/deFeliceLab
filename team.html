Principal investigator

Fernanda G. De Felice

Associate Professor · Queen's University — Centre for Neuroscience Studies, and the Departments of Biomedical & Molecular Sciences and Psychiatry.

Her work sits at the intersection of Alzheimer's disease and metabolism, with a focus on the links between Alzheimer's, diabetes and inflammation. She studies how hormones act in neurodegeneration and how insulin signalling breaks down in the Alzheimer's brain. Her finding that irisin — an exercise-linked hormone — protects against Alzheimer's has drawn wide interest from scientists and media around the world.
