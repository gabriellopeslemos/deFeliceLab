Brain Health & Smartwatch Tracking

Participation lasts about 12 months, with follow-up visits every three months at our research site in Kingston, Ontario. Your participation can help researchers better understand the factors linked to healthy cognitive aging and support the development of more accessible ways to study brain health in the future.

What participation involves

What does participation involve?

Study visits

Attend 5 study visits over 12 months at our research site in Kingston, Ontario.

Smartwatch monitoring

Use a smartwatch during the study so we can measure patterns such as sleep, activity, and other health-related signals. We'll provide one if you don't have it, along with instructions and ongoing support across the study.

Cognitive & health assessments

Complete questionnaires and cognitive assessments to help us understand different aspects of health and daily functioning.

Blood samples

Provide blood samples during study visits so researchers can examine biological markers related to brain health.

Saliva samples

Provide saliva samples during study visits so researchers can examine biological markers related to brain health.

Video-based eye tracking

Participate in video-based eye tracking sessions to help researchers understand visual attention and cognitive processes.

Questions

Frequently asked questions.

How do I know if I can join?

You may be eligible if you are 18 or older, live in or can travel to Kingston, Ontario, and are cognitively healthy, with no neurodegenerative or severe psychiatric conditions.

Does participation cost anything?

No. There is no cost to participate in the study.

Will my information be kept private?

Yes. Study data are handled confidentially and stored securely according to research ethics and institutional requirements.

Do I need to be an expert in technology?

No. The study team will explain each step and help you with the smartwatch and study procedures.

Can I ask questions before deciding?

Yes. You can contact the study team at star.defelice@gmail.com.

Ethics approval

This study has received ethical approval from the Queen's University Health Sciences and Affiliated Teaching Hospitals Research Ethics Board (HSREB).

For ethics concerns, please contact HSREB at 1-844-535-2988 (toll-free in North America) or email researchethics@queensu.ca.
